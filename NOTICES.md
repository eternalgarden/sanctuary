#### godot-cef

- **Description**: Chromium Embedded Framework (CEF) integration for Godot, powering the in-game browser.
- **Author**: Delton Ding
- **Version**: v1.17.0 (pinned in `src/moon/tools/fetch_godot_cef.sh`)
- **Changes**: None. Native binaries are fetched by the script, not tracked.
- **Source**: https://github.com/dsh0416/godot-cef
- **License**: MIT, Copyright 2025-2026 Delton Ding
- **Bundled**: CEF (BSD-3-Clause, Marshall A. Greenblatt) and Chromium, which carries its own third-party notices.

#### Proton Scatter

- **Description**: Procedural placement of props and foliage inside editable shapes, driven by a stack of modifiers.
- **Author**: HungryProton
- **Version**: 4.2.0
- **Source**: https://github.com/HungryProton/scatter
- **License**: MIT, Copyright (c) 2019 HungryProton
- **Local patches**: `src/cache/scatter_cache.gd` — `cache_file` uses `@export_file_path` and `save_cache()` resolves it with `ResourceUID.ensure_path()`, because Godot 4.4+ stores `@export_file` values as `uid://` and `ResourceSaver.save()` rejects those (error 15). Re-apply when updating.
